## 🩸 Who is Lanciuy? // The Nocturnal Engineer

I am **Lanciuy** — a Full-Stack Software Engineer, Autonomous AI Systems Architect, and Creative Technologist who builds software with the precision of an aerospace cockpit and the moody elegance of an 80s synthwave score.

While most developers rely on bloated cloud APIs and cookie-cutter frameworks, I obsess over **sovereign computing**:
- Harnessing **raw local GPUs** (CUDA, PyTorch, TensorRT, NVENC) to crush cloud latency.
- Developing **autonomous agent swarms** that plan, execute, and self-heal without human micromanagement.
- Designing **cyber-streetwear & neo-brutalist user interfaces** that give users visceral feedback, rich haptics, and zero corporate slop.

I am the architect of [**CLIPGO**](https://github.com/Lanciuy/CLIPGO) — an open-source, subprocess-isolated autonomous AI video factory that turns hours of video into viral 9:16 mobile masterpieces in 12 seconds on local hardware.
